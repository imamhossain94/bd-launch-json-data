## SPECIAL TASK — Suravi vs Surovi duplication verdict

**Verdict: three physical launches, not six; and "Surovi 10" never existed in service. Keep the "Surovi" spelling.**

1. **Spelling**: The operator's own English spelling is **Surovi** everywhere it appears in Latin script — Facebook page "**Surovi Shipping Lines**" (surovishippinglines.official), hull/videos "**M.V. SUROVI 9**" of "**Surovi Navigation CO**" (YouTube CN5eNgo33w4), booking site seatbooking.com.bd "M.V. Surovi 9", English press Dhaka Tribune & The Daily Star "MV Surovi 9". No credible source spells the launches "Suravi" ("Suravi/Surovi Paribahan" is the unrelated bus line; সুরভী is the same Bangla word both ways).
2. **Fleet size**: Company rows (official district page) list exactly **Surovi-7, Surovi-8, Surovi-9** under মেসার্স সুরভী নেভিগেশন কোং. No source lists six hulls.
3. **"Surovi 10"**: Surovi-7 burned at Sadarghat 2019-10-25, was rebuilt at Hasnabad dockyard and was announced to relaunch as **সুরভী-১০** (Dhaka Post /country/29145), but the company **reverted to the popular name Surovi-7** before re-entering service (Dhaka Post /country/73003; YouTube w1OuVW2OSdg "নতুন রূপে সেজে সার্ভিসে ফিরেছে এম ভি সুরভী-৭"). A few blogs that scraped 2021 announcements still list "Surovi (7/8/9/10)" — that is the same hull counted twice. **mv-surovi-10 is a phantom entry.**
4. **Data comparison** (digest): the suravi-* and surovi-* twins agree on route, 4 floors, 18 knots, 2 engines and overlap on every fare band; they differ only in invented precision (times 8:00–9:00 PM; suravi fares are point values ≈ the low end of surovi ranges; surovi durations 8h30m/7h30m vs suravi 6h30m — the surovi durations are closer to reality). Contacts: suravi-9 has the real Dhaka-office number 01711983534; surovi-8 has the real hotline pair 01711453989/01712772786; suravi-8's 01726200222 appears in no source.
5. **Recommended action**:
   - **Delete `mv-suravi-7`, `mv-suravi-8`, `mv-suravi-9`** (duplicates), after merging their few good fields into the kept entries (notably contact 01711983534 → Surovi-9; amenity "Life Jackets"/"Restaurant" items are generic).
   - **Delete `mv-surovi-10`** (never operated under that name).
   - **Keep `mv-surovi-7`, `mv-surovi-8`, `mv-surovi-9`** with name spelling "MV Surovi N".
   - **Operator** on kept entries: change "Surovi Group" (WRONG — unrelated conglomerate surovigroup.com) → **"Surovi Shipping Lines"** (aka Surovi Navigation Co).
