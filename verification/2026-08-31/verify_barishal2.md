# Verification: Dhaka ⇄ Barishal batch 2 (10 launches)
Verified 2026-08-31. Confidence levels: CONFIRMED (2+ independent sources), LIKELY (1 decent source), UNVERIFIED (nothing found), WRONG (sources contradict dataset; correction given).

## ROUTE-WIDE FINDINGS (apply to all 10 entries)

**1. Post-Padma-Bridge service level.** Before the bridge (Jun 2022) ~5-8 launches sailed each way daily; by 2024 only ~2 per direction per night, with owners running hulls on a ROTATION (each hull a few trips per month). ~16 services shrank to ~4-6 rotating operators. Sources: The Daily Star "Launch operators struggling to stay afloat" (https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/launch-operators-struggling-stay-afloat-3790656, via search summary; page 403s), TBS (https://www.tbsnews.net/features/panorama/sadarghats-legacy-struggles-decline-launch-service-1087601), bdnews24 (https://bdnews24.com/bangladesh/river-transporters-stare-into-an-unknown-future-as-bangladeshs-longest-bridge-appears). Consequence: every per-launch "runs daily/nightly" claim in our descriptions is overstated; hulls alternate. Launches CONFIRMED actually rostered on 28 Dec 2025 (fog-cancellation report, Prothom Alo https://www.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/district/84gjqxw1ks): **Parabat-11, Parabat-12, M Khan-7, Surovi-7, (via-route) Sundarban-12**. Sundarban-10 confirmed sailing 11 Jun 2025 (operator FB, below). One earlier report of Prince Awlad, **Parabat-18**, Surovi-7 and **Sundarban-11** cancelling a day's Dhaka-bound trips citing passenger shortage: Jugantor https://www.jugantor.com/country-news/623764 / banglanews24 https://banglanews24.com/national/news/bd/1007221.details (both paywalled/403; names via search snippets).

**2. Journey duration — dataset "5h 30m" is WRONG for all ten.** Every current source puts Sadarghat–Barishal at 7-10 h: vromonprio 2026 "৭-৮ ঘন্টা" (https://vromonprio.com/dhaka-to-barisal-launch-fare-and-schedule), infovandar 2024 & vromonguide "৮-১০ ঘণ্টা" (https://infovandar.com/... , https://vromonguide.com/how-to-go-barisal), barisalsangbad 2026 "8-9 hours overnight" with arrivals: Manami dep 8:15 PM → arr ~5:00 AM (8h45m), Sundarban dep 8:30 PM → 5:30 AM (9h), Parabat dep 9:00 PM → 6:30 AM (9h30m) (https://barisalsangbad.com/barisalsangbad-com-dhaka-barishal-launch-schedule/). Schema-fit correction: use **"8h 30m"** (Manami, M Khan 7) and **"9h 0m"** (Parabat & Sundarban hulls); the honest range is 8h-9h30m.

**3. Fare landscape 2025-2026.** Two consistent clusters: (a) operator rate-card (travelinfo/vromontrips/bdtechdiary per-launch pages): deck ৳300, sofa ৳600, single ৳1,000, double ৳2,000, family ৳2,600 (AC 3,000), deluxe ৳3,500, semi-VIP ৳4,000, VIP ৳5,000-10,000; (b) market-price blogs 2025-26: deck 350-500, single 1,200-1,800, double 2,400-3,500, family 3,000-5,500, VIP 8,000-15,000 (vromonguide top end). Our dataset values generally sit between the clusters → most fare fields = plausible/LIKELY, exceptions flagged per launch. (Post-bridge there were fare CUTS, not hikes, on this route — deck was even discounted to ৳100-200 in promo wars.)

**4. Departure times.** Rate-card/older sources: 9:00 PM for nearly all. 2026 aggregators (banglakathan https://banglakathan.com/ঢাকা-টু-বরিশাল-লঞ্চ-ভাড়া/, barisalsangbad): Manami 8:15 PM, Sundarban series 8:30 PM, Surovi 8:45 PM, Parabat series 9:00 PM, Adventure/Kirtonkhola 9:15 PM. So "09:00 PM" stands for Parabat hulls; slightly disputed for Manami/Sundarban (see entries).
