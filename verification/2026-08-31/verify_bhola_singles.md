## Naming & ghat-spelling verdicts

### Ilisha vs Elisha — SAME PLACE (data-quality flag CONFIRMED)
ইলিশা লঞ্চঘাট/ফেরিঘাট, **Bhola Sadar upazila, Bhola district**, on the Ilisha River (the Meghna's local name there). English usage in reliable sources is "Ilisha": The Daily Star "Bhola's Ilisha Ghat" [DS-ILI], Wikipedia "Ilisha River"/"Ilisha Ferry Terminal" [WP-ILI]. "Elisha" is a transliteration variant used by travelinfo.com.bd only.
**Canonical recommendation: "Ilisha"** — rename mv-zam-zam-7's route "Dhaka ⇄ Elisha" → "Dhaka ⇄ Ilisha" (and its schedule keys), matching mv-parabat-15. Optionally standardize both as "Dhaka ⇄ Ilisha (Bhola)".
